# Tabs

The Jobs tab is permanent, non-closable, and pinned first. Job URLs are canonical tab identities. Opening an existing URL selects its tab. Job tabs can be dragged to reorder them, and their order is persisted. Closing an active job tab selects its right neighbor, then its left neighbor, and finally the Jobs tab.

Every selected tab shows its own toolbar. The Jobs tab has refresh and search controls. Each job tab has refresh controls and a persisted detail-or-card view preference that defaults to detail.
